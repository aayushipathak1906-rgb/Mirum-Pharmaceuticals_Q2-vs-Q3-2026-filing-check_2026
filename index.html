How to read the trials

Endpoint. Virologic response plus normal liver enzymes (ALT) at Week 24.

Control. Both AZURE trials used a delayed-treatment arm, not placebo. It crossed over at Week 24 in AZURE-1 and Week 12 in AZURE-4.

Drug. Brelovitug is a fully human IgG1 antibody against HBsAg, given under the skin weekly or every four weeks.

Next. BLA planned for 1H 2027. AZURE-2 and AZURE-3 topline in 2H 2027.
